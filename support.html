Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

App records and imported files stay in Monthmark's local iOS container. The current version has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated cloud sync.

Why is some Health context missing?

Monthmark only reads Health categories you allow and only when data exists for the requested period. Review access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Does the personal symptom burden score diagnose PMDD or PMS?

No. It is a transparent personal comparison scale based only on recorded days. It has no clinical mild, moderate, or severe cutoffs.

How does exporting work?

You choose the date range and content, generate a fixed snapshot, and then select where to save or send it. Nothing is uploaded by Monthmark automatically.

常见问题

数据保存在哪里？

App 记录和导入文件保存在望舒的 iOS 本地容器中。当前版本没有账号、广告、分析或开发者运营的云同步。

为什么有些健康背景没有显示？

望舒只读取你允许的健康类别，而且相应时间范围内需要存在数据。可以前往 iOS“设置”→“隐私与安全性”→“健康”检查权限。

个人症状负担分数能诊断 PMDD 或 PMS 吗？

不能。它只是基于已记录日的透明个人对照尺子，没有临床轻、中、重分界。

导出是怎样工作的？

由你选择日期范围和内容，生成固定快照，再选择保存或发送位置。望舒不会自动上传。
