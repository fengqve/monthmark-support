Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 7, 2026

Monthmark is provided by the independent developer Fengqve ("we"). This policy explains how the Monthmark iOS app handles information.

1. Information processed by the app

Monthmark can process the following information only to provide features you choose to use:

  • Symptom records: pain intensity and locations, daily function, emotional burden, sleep impact, other physical symptoms, relief methods, notes, and timestamps you enter.
  • Health data you authorize: menstrual flow, sleep analysis, steps, flights climbed, active energy, workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, and sleeping wrist temperature. Monthmark requests read-only HealthKit access and does not write data to Health.
  • Care documents you choose: photos, scans, or files such as medicine packaging, prescriptions, medical records, and test reports, plus text recognized on device and corrections you confirm.
  • Device preferences: local reminder time, notification authorization state, and app settings.
  • Optional bedtime protection: with separate Screen Time permission, the app uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to schedule and apply app and website shields on this device. Bedtime settings and protection state stay on device and are not uploaded to the developer.

2. Local processing and storage

Monthmark does not require an account and does not send your symptom, HealthKit, or care-document data to a developer-operated server. App records and imported files are stored in the app's local iOS container. Text recognition is performed with Apple's on-device frameworks.

The current version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated cloud synchronization.

3. Sharing and exports

Monthmark does not sell personal data. Information leaves the app only when you deliberately use an export or share action and select a destination. The selected destination, such as Files, Mail, Messages, or another app, handles that copy under its own privacy terms.

4. Permissions and your choices

You decide whether to grant Health, camera, photo-library, file, notification, and optional Screen Time access. You can change or revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Monthmark continues to work with reduced features when optional permissions are not granted. You can stop bedtime protection from Monthmark and revoke its Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device while the app and its data remain installed. Removing Monthmark removes its local app container according to iOS behavior. Copies you exported remain wherever you chose to save or send them and must be deleted there separately. Monthmark creates no online account or developer-server copy, so there is no server-side account data to delete.

6. Medical and children's privacy

Monthmark is a personal record-keeping tool, not a medical device or diagnostic service. It is not directed to children under 13. If symptoms are severe, sudden, or concerning, seek professional medical care.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated. For privacy questions, open a support request using the link below. Please do not include medical records or sensitive health details in a public issue.

望舒隐私政策

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

望舒由独立开发者 Fengqve（下称“我们”）提供。本政策说明望舒 iOS App 如何处理信息。

一、App 处理的信息

望舒只为提供你主动选择使用的功能，处理以下信息：

  • 症状记录：你填写的疼痛强度与位置、生活功能、情绪负担、睡眠影响、其他身体感受、缓解方式、备注和时间。
  • 你授权的健康数据：经期、睡眠、步数、爬楼、活动能量、锻炼、心率、静息心率、心率变异性和睡眠腕温。望舒仅申请读取 HealthKit，不会向“健康”写入数据。
  • 你选择的就医资料：药盒、处方、病历、检查报告等照片、扫描件或文件，以及在本机识别的文字和你确认的更正。
  • 设备偏好：本机提醒时间、通知授权状态和 App 设置。
  • 可选睡前保护：在你单独授予屏幕使用时间权限后，App 使用 Apple 的 Family Controls、Managed Settings 和 Device Activity 框架，在本机安排并执行 App 与网站保护。睡前设置和保护状态留在设备上，不会上传给开发者。

二、本机处理与存储

望舒不要求注册账号，也不会把症状、HealthKit 或就医资料发送到开发者运营的服务器。记录和导入文件保存在 App 的 iOS 本地容器中，文字识别使用 Apple 的本机框架完成。

当前版本不含广告、第三方分析、追踪 SDK，也不使用开发者运营的云同步。

三、分享与导出

望舒不会出售个人数据。只有当你主动使用导出或分享功能并选择接收位置时，信息才会离开 App。你选择的“文件”、邮件、信息或其他 App 会按其自身隐私规则处理该副本。

四、权限与选择

是否允许健康、相机、照片、文件、通知和可选屏幕使用时间权限由你决定。你可以在 iOS“设置”中更改或撤销权限；不授予可选权限时，望舒仍可使用其余功能。你可以在望舒中停止睡前保护，也可以在 iOS“设置”中撤销它的屏幕使用时间权限。

五、保留与删除

只要 App 及其数据仍安装在设备上，本地数据就会留在设备中。删除望舒后，iOS 会按系统机制删除它的本地 App 容器。已经导出的副本仍保留在你选择的位置，需要在那里另行删除。望舒不创建在线账号或开发者服务器副本，因此没有需要向服务器申请删除的账号数据。

六、医疗与儿童隐私

望舒是个人记录工具，不是医疗器械或诊断服务，也不面向 13 岁以下儿童。如果症状严重、突然出现或让你担忧，请及时寻求专业医疗帮助。

七、政策更新与联系

App 功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并修改上方生效日期。如有隐私问题，请通过下方链接提交支持请求。请不要在公开 issue 中填写病历或敏感健康信息。
